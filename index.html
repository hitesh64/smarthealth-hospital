    Emergency Ambulance

    Accident or life-threatening emergency? Press SOS — your live location goes to the hospital automatically and you can track the ambulance.

    Call +91 1800-123-4567

    Request an ambulance to an address

    What is the emergency?

    Filled in from your profile. Change anything if the ambulance is for someone else.

    What happens next

    1. 1
      The hospital gets your request

      It appears on the emergency desk as soon as you confirm.

    2. 2
      An ambulance is dispatched

      The desk sends the ambulance to the address you gave.

    3. 3
      You can follow the status

      It changes from “Awaiting dispatch” to “Dispatched” in My Appointments.

    While you wait

    • Keep this phone free so the driver can reach you.
    • Send someone to the gate or the road to guide the ambulance.
    • Keep the patient's ID, medical papers and current medicines ready.
