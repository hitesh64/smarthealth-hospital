Medicine Orders

Each action moves an order one step: Confirm, Packed, Out for Delivery or Ready for Pickup, then Delivered or Collected. The patient is emailed at every step, and cancelling returns the medicines to stock. For a UPI order, verify the payment (UTR) in your UPI account before confirming.
